Open my office computer

How it works

  1. Click Open my office computer.
  2. Type the Windows user name and password you use on that computer. DishNet does not store them.
  3. The office desktop appears in this tab. Open Tally there, as at the office.
  4. When you finish: close the company in Tally, then click Disconnect at the top.

One person at a time can use a normal Windows PC. If a colleague is working on it, Windows will ask whether to disconnect them.
